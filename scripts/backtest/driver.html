Backtest driver

Run by scripts/backtest/replay.py, which serves the repo, hands this page a job at /__job and collects results at /__result. For each historical fixture it loads index.html?fixture=…#selftest (no live data), turns a handful of the model's constants into parameters, and for each configuration recomputes the board and reports the forecasts, rest-of-season values and Waivers moves.
