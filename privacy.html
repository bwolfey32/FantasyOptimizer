Benny’s Picks

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

Benny’s Picks (“we”) is a fantasy football lineup tool at bennyspicks.us, run by Ben Wolfe. This page explains what we collect and why. Questions: benjimanwolfe@gmail.com.

Without an account

Your teams, settings and saved lineups are stored only in your own browser (its local storage). We don’t receive them. To show projections, your browser loads public data directly from Sleeper, ESPN and Open-Meteo, so those services see a normal web request from you, as they would when you visit them.

With an account

  • What we store: your email address, your teams (rosters, scoring, lineup slots and league links), your settings and overrides, and Benny’s weekly lineups for your teams. This lets you see them on every device you sign in on.
  • Where: in our database hosted by Supabase. Each person can read only their own data.
  • Sign-in: by a one-time code emailed to you, or with Google. With Google, we receive your name and email address from Google; we don’t receive your Google password.
  • Deleting: Sign out removes your data from that browser. Delete account (in the Account panel) permanently deletes your account and everything stored with it.

Payments (Pro)

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them. We keep a record of your plan (monthly or season pass), its status and when it ends, plus the Stripe customer ID that links your account to your payments. Stripe’s own privacy policy covers what Stripe collects.

Advertising

Free users see ads. Until our advertising partner is set up, these are only our own promotions for Pro, which use no cookies or tracking. Once we use Google AdSense, Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. Pro members see no ads, and the ad code isn’t loaded for them.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t use analytics or tracking scripts beyond the advertising described above.

Children

Benny’s Picks isn’t intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
