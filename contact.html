Email is the best way to reach us, for anything about the site. We read every message.

What to write about

  • Help with your lineup or team setup: a league that won’t import, a player who looks wrong, or a projection that doesn’t make sense.
  • Your account: trouble signing in, or questions about what’s stored. You can delete your account yourself from the Account panel; to have your analytics deleted too, email us.
  • Pro and billing: a charge you don’t recognize, a duplicate charge, or help cancelling. See the Terms of Service for how Pro and refunds work.
  • Privacy requests: questions about the data described in our Privacy Policy, or a request to see or delete it.
  • Feedback and ideas: bugs, features you’d like, or anything that was confusing.
  • Advertising and partnerships.

Help us help you

For a problem with your team, it helps to include:

  • Your league platform (Sleeper or ESPN) and scoring format.
  • What you expected and what you saw, with a screenshot if you can.
  • Your phone or computer and browser.

Please don’t send passwords or card details. We never need them.
