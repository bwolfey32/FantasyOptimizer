Benny's Picks audit

Drafts whole leagues from a data bundle (ESPN roster percentages as the draft order), runs Lineup, Start/Sit, Waivers, Benny's Moves and Benny Grade for every team under several league setups, and flags advice a fantasy player would call wrong on sight. ?fixture=path picks the bundle (default: snapshot.json, the live data), ?leagues=N how many leagues. Lines starting with ERROR are rule breaks; WARN lines are worth a look. Run it with scripts/audit.py, or serve the repo folder and open this page.
